## FORMAL MANAGEMENT BODY CYBERSECURITY OVERSIGHT & COMPLIANCE RESOLUTION
*Mandatory documentation under Directive (EU) 2022/2555 to establish Board supervision, training, and personal liability mitigation.*

---

### LEGAL CONTEXT & STATUTORY DUTY
Pursuant to **Article 20(1) of Directive (EU) 2022/2555 (NIS2)**:
> *"The management bodies of essential and important entities shall approve the cybersecurity risk-management measures taken by those entities in order to comply with Article 21, oversee their implementation and can be held liable for infringement by the entities of those obligations."*

Furthermore, under **Article 20(2)**:
> *"Members of the management bodies shall follow training and shall offer similar training to their employees on a regular basis."*
